A school gains access to the Apps by subscribing through the School Admin app. Subscription plans, pricing, and duration (e.g. 1-year, 3-year, or 5-year plans) are shown within the School Admin app at the time of subscribing and may be updated by us from time to time. 3. Student Capacity and Subject Registration Codes

Each school's subscription includes capacity for up to 120 students. Access for students beyond this number is provided through Subject Registration Codes, issued as follows:

  • Each code issued covers up to 60 students.
  • A school's subscription includes an initial allocation of codes covering its base capacity.
  • If a school needs to register more students, up to the 120-student limit, the school may request an additional code.
  • Requests for a code are made directly to us (currently via WhatsApp), not automatically through the App.
  • Codes are generated and sent to the school's registered email by our team once a request is confirmed.
  • Requests beyond the 120-student limit are treated as an increase in the school's usage and are subject to an additional charge, to be agreed with the school before any extra capacity is provided.

This process is currently handled manually by our team and is not automatically tracked or enforced within the App itself. We may automate this process in the future, at which point these Terms will be updated to reflect that change.

4. Code Validity and Reassignment

A Subject Registration Code is valid for use by the school it was issued to only. If a school's subscription becomes inactive (e.g. lapses without renewal), a previously issued code may, at our discretion, be deactivated or made available for reassignment to another school.
