Privacy Policy — WAEC LMS School Admin

Last updated: August 25, 2026

WAEC LMS School Admin (“the App”) is developed by Cj-Tech for use by school administrators of participating schools to manage their school's subscription, register and approve staff/students, oversee timetables and results, and message teachers and students. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you register or use the App, we collect:

  • Administrator identity information: your full name, email address, and phone number.
  • School information: school name, school ID, and school-level configuration and records.
  • Student and teacher data: as part of your role administering your school, the App gives you access to your school's registered students' and teachers' academic and account records (names, contact details, scores, timetables, subject registrations).
  • Subscription and payment-related information: your school's subscription plan and status, and details you submit to confirm a bank transfer payment — specifically, the last 4 digits of the transaction/receipt reference. We do not collect full card numbers, full account numbers, or banking passwords through the App.
  • Messages: the content of messages you send to or receive from teachers and students within the App.
  • Device/session data: login tokens used to keep you signed in, and basic connectivity status used to sync your data when you're back online.

We do not collect camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location data — the App does not request or use these permissions.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in across sessions.
  • To let you manage your school's registered students, teachers, timetables, and results.
  • To process and confirm your school's subscription payment, and to communicate with you about your subscription status.
  • To enable messaging between you, your school's teachers, and students.
  • To improve reliability of the App, including caching data locally so it keeps working with a poor or no internet connection, then syncing once you're back online.

3. Payments

School subscriptions are paid via direct bank transfer to an account we provide, or, where available, through an online payment processor. When paying by bank transfer, you submit the last 4 digits of your transaction/receipt reference so our team can manually confirm and match your payment; this reference, along with your school name, school ID, selected plan, and your phone/email, is sent to our team to process the subscription. We do not store or have access to your full bank account number, card details, or online banking credentials. Where an online payment processor is used instead, that transaction is handled directly by the payment processor under its own privacy and security terms.

4. Access to School, Teacher, and Student Data

As a school administrator, the App grants you access to records belonging to your school's registered teachers and students — this is a core function of the App, provided so you can carry out normal school administration duties (registration, approvals, oversight of results and timetables). This data must only be used for legitimate school administration purposes, and access is limited to your own school's records.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is transmitted to our servers over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored in a secured cloud database. Login credentials are never stored in plain text. Some data is cached locally on your device solely so the App can function while offline, and is synced back to our servers once your connection is restored.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, your school's information, or your students'/teachers' information to third parties. Data is visible only to authorized staff at your school, our support team (for the purpose of account and subscription support), and, where applicable, the payment processor handling an online transaction. We do not share information with advertisers.

7. Data Retention

Your school's information is retained for as long as your school maintains an account with WAEC LMS, including a reasonable period after a subscription lapses to allow renewal. If you would like data removed, contact us using the details below.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal or school data by contacting us at the email below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
